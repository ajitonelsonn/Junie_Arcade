# How Junie Arcade Works

**A comprehensive guide to playing, competing, and winning in Junie's Arcade!**

---

## 🎮 Overview

Junie's Arcade is a **multi-game tournament experience** featuring three fast-paced mini-games designed for competitive play at Cloud9 and JetBrains events. Players compete for high scores, earn Champion Points, and climb the global leaderboard.

**Perfect for**: Event booths, quick gaming sessions, competitive tournaments
**Duration**: 2-5 minutes per tournament run
**Platform**: Web-based (desktop recommended)

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Step 1: Enter Your Information

When you first enter any game from the Main Arena:

1. **Enter your Name** (3-20 characters)
2. **Select your Country** from 250+ countries
3. **Start the Game**

After finishing your first game:

- 🎯 **See your rank** compared to other players
- ➡️ **Click "Continue"** to play the next game
- 🏆 **Complete all 3 games** to maximize your Champion Points

### Step 4: Share Your Achievement

Once you've played all 3 games:

- 🎴 **Download your Achievement Card** with your final ranking
- 📤 **Share on social media** to show off your skills
- 🔄 **Play again** to improve your score (new session with new Player ID)

## 🎯 The Three Games

### 🚀 Jump Master

**Type**: Endless Runner / Platformer
**Duration**: 50 seconds
**Goal**: Collect items and survive as long as possible
**Score Range**: 0 - 3,000+ points

#### How to Play:

**Controls:**

- **Desktop**: Press `SPACE` to jump (press twice for double jump)
- **Mobile**: Tap the screen to jump (tap twice for double jump)

**Gameplay:**

- Run automatically from left to right
- Jump over bug obstacles to avoid game over
- Collect **Cloud9 logos** (50 points) and **Coins** (20 points)
- Every 10 meters traveled = 1 point (distance bonus)
- Every 100 meters = +25 bonus points (milestone)
- Game ends when: time runs out (victory) or you hit a bug (game over)

**Scoring:**

```
Final Score = Collectibles + Distance Bonus + Milestones

Example at 350m with 15 coins, 8 Cloud9 logos:
- Collectibles: (15 × 20) + (8 × 50) = 700 pts
- Distance: 350 ÷ 10 = 35 pts
- Milestones: 3 × 25 = 75 pts
Total: 810 points
```

**Strategy Tips:**

- 🎯 Prioritize Cloud9 logos (50 pts) over coins (20 pts)
- 🔄 Master double jump for high collectibles
- ⏱️ Survival = points (distance + milestones)
- 🚀 Late game gets faster - stay focused!

**Difficulty Progression:**

- **0-20s**: Moderate speed (500 px/s), building momentum
- **20-35s**: Fast speed (580 px/s), dense patterns
- **35-50s**: EXTREME speed (700 px/s), maximum intensity

---

### ⚡ Reflex Arena

**Type**: Target Clicking / Reaction Game
**Duration**: 50 seconds
**Goal**: Click good targets, avoid bad ones, build combos
**Score Range**: 0 - 5,000+ points

#### How to Play:

**Controls:**

- **Desktop/Mobile**: Click or tap on targets

**Good Targets** (click these!):

- ⭐ **Star**: 10 points
- 🪙 **Coin**: 20 points
- 💎 **Gem**: 30 points
- 🏆 **Trophy**: 50 points

**Bad Targets** (avoid these!):

- 🐛 **Bug**: -20 points + combo reset
- 🦠 **Virus**: -20 points + combo reset
- 💣 **Bomb**: -20 points + combo reset

**Scoring System:**

```
Points = Base Value × Reaction Bonus × Combo Multiplier

Where:
- Reaction Bonus: 2× if clicked within 250ms, otherwise 1×
- Combo Multiplier: 1× to 5× (builds with consecutive good targets)

Example: Trophy + fast click + 5× combo
50 × 2 × 5 = 500 points per click!
```

**Strategy Tips:**

- 🔥 Build your combo to 5× as fast as possible
- ⚡ Click within 250ms for 2× bonus (look for "FAST!" indicator)
- 🎯 At max combo, prioritize trophies (500 pts each!)
- 🚫 One mistake resets combo - accuracy matters
- 👀 Use peripheral vision to scan the whole screen

**Difficulty Progression:**

- **0-10s**: Warmup (600ms spawn rate)
- **10-30s**: Acceleration (targets spawn faster)
- **30-50s**: INSANE (300ms spawn rate, 6-8 targets on screen!)

---

### 🧠 Memory Match

**Type**: Card Matching / Memory Puzzle
**Duration**: 100 seconds
**Goal**: Match all 8 pairs of champion cards
**Score Range**: 800 - 4,500+ points

#### How to Play:

**Controls:**

- **Desktop/Mobile**: Click or tap cards to flip them

**Game Rules:**

- 16 cards (8 pairs of League/Valorant champions)
- Flip 2 cards at a time to find matches
- Matched pairs stay revealed
- Wrong pairs flip back after 1 second
- Each pair of flips = 1 move

**Scoring System:**

**Match Points** (progressive combo):

```
1st match: 75 × 1 = 75 points
2nd consecutive: 75 × 2 = 150 points
3rd consecutive: 75 × 3 = 225 points
4th consecutive: 75 × 4 = 300 points
5th+ consecutive: 75 × 5 = 375 points (max)

Missing a match resets combo to 1×
```

**Always-Applied Bonuses:**

- **Time Bonus**: Remaining seconds × 15
- **Accuracy Bonus**: (16 ÷ Total Moves) × 150
- **Combo Bonus**: Best combo × 25

**Completion-Only Bonuses** (if all 8 pairs matched):

- **Completion Bonus**: +500 points
- **Perfect Game Bonus**: +300 points (exactly 16 moves)
- **Speed Bonus**:
  - 60+ seconds remaining: +400 points
  - 40-59 seconds remaining: +200 points

**Strategy Tips:**

- 🧠 Memorize card positions in first 30-40 seconds
- 🔥 Build and maintain combos (5× = 375 pts per match!)
- ⏱️ Speed matters: 60+ seconds = 400 bonus
- 🎯 Perfect game (16 moves) = 300 bonus
- 💪 Even incomplete runs earn bonuses!

**Achievement Thresholds:**

- 🌟 Perfect Memory: 16 moves
- ⚡ Speed Runner: Complete with 60+ seconds
- 🔥 Combo Master: Achieve 5× combo
- 🎯 High Scorer: Complete in 20 moves or less

## 🏆 Tournament Flow

### How Sessions Work

1. **Start Anywhere**: Choose any of the 3 games to begin
2. **Continue the Mission**: After game 1, click "Continue" for game 2
3. **Complete the Trilogy**: Finish all 3 games in one session
4. **Automatic Progression**: The system guides you to unplayed games

### Suggested Path (Optional)

The default flow is: **Jump Master → Reflex Arena → Memory Match**

But you can start with **any game** - the system adapts!

### Already Played Protection

**Important**: You can only play each game **once per session**

- ✅ If you try to replay a game you already completed, it will be locked
- ✅ This prevents duplicate scores and ensures fair competition
- ✅ To play again, finish your session and return to Main Arena (new session starts)

### Tournament Completion

Once you've completed all 3 games:

1. **Overall Rank Calculated**: Your Champion Points are totaled
2. **Achievement Card Available**: Download your personalized card
3. **Leaderboard Position**: See where you rank globally
4. **Merchandise Eligibility**: Top 3 at GDC events win prizes!

## 🔄 Session Management

### How Sessions Work:

**Starting a New Session:**

1. Enter your name and country
2. Unique Player ID is generated
3. Credentials are locked for this session
4. You can now play all 3 games

**During Your Session:**

- ✅ Name and country are locked (cannot change)
- ✅ Player ID tracks your progress across games
- ✅ Each game can only be played once
- ✅ Scores are automatically saved to leaderboard

**Ending Your Session:**

- Click "Back to Arena" or "Exit to Main Menu"
- Session ends, credentials are cleared
- You can start a new session anytime!

### Playing Again for Better Scores:

Want to improve your ranking? Here's how:

1. **Finish your current session** (or exit to main menu)
2. **Start a new session** with the same name/country
3. **A new unique Player ID is generated**
4. **Play all games again** for potentially better scores
5. **Both attempts appear on leaderboard** as separate entries

**Important**: Each session is tracked independently. Your best overall performance counts!

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I change my name/country during a session?

No, credentials are locked when you start playing to ensure fair competition and consistent scoring.

### Can I play the same game twice in one session?

No, each game can only be played once per session. To play again, finish your session and start a new one from the Main Arena.

### Do I have to play all 3 games?

No, but playing all 3 games maximizes your Champion Points and overall leaderboard ranking. Players who complete all 3 games always rank higher than those who only play 1 or 2.

### How often does the leaderboard update?

The leaderboard auto-refreshes every 10 seconds with the latest scores from all players globally.

### Can I compete by country?

Yes! You can filter leaderboards by country to see how you rank against players from your nation.

### What if I want to improve my score?

Return to the Main Arena to end your current session, then start a new session. Each attempt gets a unique Player ID, and both scores appear on the leaderboard.

### How do I download my achievement card?

Complete all 3 games, then click "Generate Card" on the Game Over screen. Customize if desired, then click "Download Card" to save the PNG image.

### Is my data saved?

Yes! Scores are automatically saved to the database when you complete each game. Your achievement card can also be uploaded to the cloud for sharing.

### Can I share my achievement card?

Absolutely! Download the card and share it on social media to show off your skills and compete with friends.
