# How the Game Works

Welcome to Junie's Arcade! This guide explains how to play the games, how sessions work, and how the tournament flow is structured.

## 1. Getting Started

When you first enter a game from the Main Arena, you will be asked to enter your **Name** and select your **Country**.

- **Identity Generation:** The system generates a unique Player ID for your current session.
- **Locking Credentials:** Your name and country are locked for the duration of the session to ensure consistent scoring.
- **Repeat Play:** If you return to the main menu and start again with the same name, a brand new unique ID will be generated. This allows you to try for a better overall tournament score without overwriting your previous attempts.

## 2. The Games

There are three main challenges in the arcade:

### Jump Master 🚀
- **Goal:** Run and jump to avoid obstacles for as long as possible.
- **Controls:** Press **SPACE** or **CLICK** to jump.
- **Scoring:** Your score is based on the distance you travel.

### Reflex Arena ⚡
- **Goal:** Click on "Good" targets as fast as possible while avoiding "Bad" ones.
- **Good Targets:** Stars, Coins, Gems, and Trophies.
- **Bad Targets:** Viruses, Bugs, and Bombs.
- **Controls:** **CLICK** or **TAP** on the targets.
- **Scoring:** Fast clicks and combos increase your score. Clicking bad targets reduces your points and resets your combo.

### Memory Match 🧠
- **Goal:** Find all matching pairs of cards before the timer runs out.
- **Controls:** **CLICK** or **TAP** to flip cards.
- **Scoring:** You get points for every match, plus bonuses for remaining time and high accuracy (fewer moves).

## 3. Tournament Flow

The arcade is designed as a multi-game tournament.

1. **Start Anywhere:** You can begin your session with any of the three games.
2. **Continue the Mission:** After completing a game, a "Continue" button will appear on the Game Over screen. It will automatically point you to the next game you haven't played yet in your current session.
3. **Sequence:** The suggested path is **Jump Master → Reflex Arena → Memory Match**, but it cycles depending on where you start.
4. **Already Played Protection:** If you try to go back to a game you already played in the current session, it will be locked to prevent duplicate scores.
5. **Tournament Completion:** Once you've played all three games, you can generate your **Overall Leaderboard Card** to see your final ranking.

## 4. Leaderboard & Rankings

- **Champion Points:** Since each game has different scoring, we convert your rank in each game into "Champion Points".
- **Overall Ranking:** Your total Champion Points across all three games determine your position on the Overall Leaderboard.
- **Priority:** Players who complete all 3 games always rank higher than those who only play 1 or 2 games.
